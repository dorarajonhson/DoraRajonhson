Selected work

Projects

ML · Microbiome · Disease
Random Forest modelling of WSSV resistance in shrimp gut microbiota
Applied Random Forest (randomForest, R) in a network-based meta-analysis to identify microbial taxa associated with White Spot Syndrome Virus resistance and susceptibility. Used taxon-level feature importance to extract key disease signals from a multi-study dataset.
Random Forest R 16S rRNA Feature importance Meta-analysis
Transcriptomics · Multi-omics
Host transcriptomic response to dietary mycotoxin contamination in shrimp
End-to-end RNA-seq pipeline (Illumina NovaSeq) on shrimp intestine and hepatopancreas: QC, alignment, DESeq2 differential expression, GO/KEGG enrichment. Paired with parallel 16S rRNA microbiome profiling — a multi-omics dataset linking host response to microbial community shifts.
RNA-seq DESeq2 GO/KEGG 16S rRNA HPC/SLURM
→ Under review · Food and Chemical Toxicology
Temporal Analysis · Community Dynamics
13-week longitudinal microbiome dynamics in closed aquaculture systems
Weekly 16S rRNA sampling of shrimp intestines over 13 weeks with daily water parameter recording. Applied the Buckley et al. (2021) framework: PCoA ordination trajectories, PERMANOVA, turnover rates, synchrony analysis, zeta diversity, and mean Bray-Curtis dissimilarity to characterise community assembly processes.
QIIME2/DADA2 phyloseq Zeta diversity Synchrony RDA LEfSe
→ Under review · Aquaculture
Metagenomics · Viromics
First metagenomic & viromic characterisation of bat bug and swallow bug microbiomes
Sequenced both DNA and RNA fractions across two ectoparasite species and three life stages. Taxonomic profiling with Kraken2/Bracken, diversity analyses, and multivariate statistics to identify community drivers, flag zoonotic taxa, and screen for AMR genes. RNA fraction enabled RNA virus detection.
Kraken2/Bracken BWA samtools fastp AMR profiling Viromics
→ Under review · PLOS Neglected Tropical Diseases
Supervised Classification · Published
ANN-based species classification from morphometric data in Cimicidae
Applied geometric morphometrics alongside ANN-based classification (XYOM) and discriminant analysis to discriminate bed bug, bat bug, and swallow bug species from pronotum shape data. Compared traditional linear morphometry to outline-based geometric approaches.
ANN classification Geometric morphometrics Discriminant analysis XYOM
→ Published · Insects 2022, 13(12):1155
Predictive Modelling · Published
GLM dose-response modelling of fipronil against plague flea vectors
Evaluated fipronil as a systemic insecticide against Xenopsylla cheopis, the primary plague vector in Madagascar. Built GLM with binomial distribution and logit-link in R to compute LD50 and LD90 values across three experimental delivery methods. Findings informed Madagascar's National Plague Control Program.
GLM / logit-link R Dose-response modelling LD50 / LD90
→ Published · J. Medical Entomology 2017
